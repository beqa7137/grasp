# Reliability widget: what the checks and the critique found, and what changed

This is the design's own first round, before the two independent critics. Part A lists what the mockup's screenshot and
collision rounds found (screenshots read at 1280, 390 and 320 px in both themes; `shoot.cjs` and its sweeps). Part B is
the critique of the first complete design against the bar (Stripe, Linear, Observable, NYT graphics, Bartosz
Ciechanowski; Load Balancing, Data Modeling, Consistency & Quorums and Latency & Queueing as the house references), as
two passes: craft, then teaching and honesty. Part C lists what was considered and kept, with the reason. The round ends
with zero findings over 174 screenshots (29 states), 11,340 setting renders (every one of 648 settings at rest, through
a free play and with ghosts), 324 experiment renders and 780 no-answer renders with six planted leaks caught, every
page sentence of every setting, and `enum.cjs` passing 282,878 checks.

## C. Considered and kept

| Considered                                                                                                    | Kept, because                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Handles on the diagram for the copies (as Load Balancing's rack edge)                                         | Four columns of 45 px on a 320 phone cannot hold a − and a + of 44 px each; steppers outside the stage are the accessible form and match Latency & Queueing's controls                                                       |
| A sampled chance overlap somewhere in the year                                                                | A pair overlaps by chance once in 57 years: a year without one is the honest typical year, and its absence is the lesson; the model's 32 s is on the scale and in the words                                                  |
| A year drawn to scale beside the ticks (a sliver for 17.5 h)                                                  | It is under a pixel at every setting and width; the nines scale is the picture of how small, and the label says ticks are not lengths                                                                                        |
| One zone for the whole chain as the shared cause                                                              | A cause shared across parts breaks the parts' independence and the series rule's exactness; shared failures are per part, which is said, and E4's reveal names zones, deploys and cells                                       |
| A third failover level (30 s) and per-part levels                                                             | 648 settings already; the challenge's reveal names the faster switch and fewer shared causes as the next nine in words, and the lesson text carries the rest                                                                 |
| A stage under 1,000 px on a 320 phone (Latency & Queueing is 985)                                             | 1,032: each of the three sections carries its own idea, the chain and the year (where every play happens) fit one screen, and the reserves that remain are the longest sentences at 232 px                                   |
| An experiment of its own for the optional dependency                                                          | It would repeat E1's arithmetic in reverse (35 h to 26.3 h); the challenge needs it, and its reveal says why                                                                                                                 |
| Error budgets as their own picture                                                                            | The headline's 30 day figure ties to the lesson's 43.2 minutes; Observability's `error-budget` widget (R45) is the place for burn rates                                                                                       |
