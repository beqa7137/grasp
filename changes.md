# Latency widget: what the checks and the critique found, and what changed

Round one (parts A to C) is the design's own first pass. Part A lists what the mockup's own screenshot and collision
rounds found (screenshots read at 1280, 390 and 320 px in both themes; `shoot.cjs` and its sweeps). Part B lists the
critique of the first complete design against the bar (Stripe, Linear, Observable, NYT graphics, Bartosz Ciechanowski;
Load Balancing, Data Modeling and Consistency & Quorums as the house references), as two passes: craft, then teaching and
honesty. Part C lists the items considered and kept, with the reason. Round one ended with zero collisions over 162
screenshots, 2,040 setting renders, 187 experiment renders and 330 no-answer renders, and `enum.cjs` passing 2,002 checks.

Round two (parts D to G) is two independent critics' review of round one. Part D maps the first critic's 23 items and
part E the second critic's 29 to what changed; part F lists what this round's own checks found on the way; part G the
two items applied in an adapted form, with the reason. Every must and should is applied, and every nice but one, which
another item superseded (D21). The mockup ends with zero findings over 180 screenshots (30 states), 5,064 setting renders
(every setting at rest, through a free play and with both kinds of ghost), 297 experiment renders, 920 no-answer renders
with four planted leaks caught, and every page sentence of all 364 settings; `enum.cjs` passes 2,348 checks.

## C. Considered and kept

| Item                                                                                                       | Why kept                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| p99 in the critical hue                                                                                    | The tail is where overload shows first, which is the critical role's meaning (overloaded); it carries a dash as its second encoding and a name, and the only other critical marks (over capacity) mean the same thing at its limit                  |
| Random service is exponential (M/M), so some requests take under 1 ms                                     | The lesson's formulas (S / (1 − u), 4.6×) are M/M/1's; the marginal counts the few under 1 ms ("3 under 1 ms") rather than hiding them, and steady work is the other extreme                                                                         |
| The sample is chosen for typicality                                                                        | Disclosed in the brief; the sample is never quoted as a model number, and common random numbers keep it monotone in load, so moving the handle never shows less queueing at more load                                                               |
| A 4 s window over capacity, against 400 ms otherwise                                                       | A ramp needs the window many times (λ + μ) / (λ − μ)²; the label says so ("The first 4 s, starting empty")                                                                                                                                       |
| E2 and E5 share the knee                                                                                   | E2 is the knee in load; E5 is the knee from a lost server, with an answer (no steady state) no other question reaches                                                                                                                             |
| No time scrubber                                                                                            | Play is the transport; at rest the band shows the most telling moment and the timeline every moment, so Reduced motion loses nothing; a scrubber would add a second handle and its hit-box checks for little                                        |
