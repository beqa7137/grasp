# Latency widget: what the checks and the critique found, and what changed

Part A lists what the mockup's own screenshot and collision rounds found (screenshots read at 1280, 390 and 320 px in
both themes; `shoot.cjs` and its sweeps). Part B lists the critique of the first complete design against the bar
(Stripe, Linear, Observable, NYT graphics, Bartosz Ciechanowski; Load Balancing, Data Modeling and Consistency & Quorums
as the house references), as two passes: craft, then teaching and honesty. Part C lists the items considered and kept,
with the reason. Every must and should is applied; the mockup ends with zero collisions over 162 screenshots, 2,040
setting renders, 187 experiment renders and 330 no-answer renders, and `enum.cjs` passes 2,002 checks.

## B. Critique

### Craft

| #   | Sev.   | Issue                                                                                                                                         | What changed                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| B1  | must   | Today's widget is the outcome only: no request, no queue, no waiting on the stage                                                            | The stage leads with the cause: a sample's band (the line, the servers, each request in service with its progress) and its timeline (arrivals, the queue's length, each server's busy time), then the model                        |
| B2  | must   | The sample and the model were not told apart: a reader could take the drawn queue for the model's                                           | Two labelled halves split by a rule ("A sample of random arrivals", "The last 400 ms of this sample"; "Response time · exact, from the model"); the sample is never summarized as a statistic                                     |
| B3  | should | The band and the timeline were two pictures of one sample with no link                                                                       | The dotted moment line, the ring on the peak and "drawn above: the longest queue, 8" tie the band to its moment                                                                                                                  |
| B4  | should | The chart's numbers and the 100 requests sat apart                                                                                            | Dashed leaders from the cursor's dots run into p50 and p99 ticks across the marginal, so the curves visibly are the 50th and 99th of the 100                                                                                     |
| B5  | should | One request's time was never drawn, only the average's                                                                                       | "Last to leave: waited 97 ms, then 54 ms of work" as a small bar in the model bar's colours, over the model's average bar                                                                                                         |
| B6  | should | The load row said the arrival rate but not what each request costs                                                                           | "70 requests/s at random, 10 ms each on average" ("exactly 10 ms each" for steady work)                                                                                                                                          |
| B7  | nice   | The dock's held state and the challenge's status before it is met showed "Not yet:"                                                         | The house: no line until met, then "Goal met:" and "You met it; now:"                                                                                                                                                            |

## C. Considered and kept

| Item                                                                                                       | Why kept                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| p99 in the critical hue                                                                                    | The tail is where overload shows first, which is the critical role's meaning (overloaded); it carries a dash as its second encoding and a name, and the only other critical marks (over capacity) mean the same thing at its limit                  |
| Random service is exponential (M/M), so some requests take under 1 ms                                     | The lesson's formulas (S / (1 − u), 4.6×) are M/M/1's; the marginal counts the few under 1 ms ("3 under 1 ms") rather than hiding them, and steady work is the other extreme                                                                         |
| The sample is chosen for typicality                                                                        | Disclosed in the brief; the sample is never quoted as a model number, and common random numbers keep it monotone in load, so moving the handle never shows less queueing at more load                                                               |
| A 4 s window over capacity, against 400 ms otherwise                                                       | A ramp needs the window many times (λ + μ) / (λ − μ)²; the label says so ("The first 4 s, starting empty")                                                                                                                                       |
| E2 and E5 share the knee                                                                                   | E2 is the knee in load; E5 is the knee from a lost server, with an answer (no steady state) no other question reaches                                                                                                                             |
| No time scrubber                                                                                            | Play is the transport; at rest the band shows the most telling moment and the timeline every moment, so Reduced motion loses nothing; a scrubber would add a second handle and its hit-box checks for little                                        |
