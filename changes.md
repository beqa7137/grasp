# Sharding widget: what the checks and the self-critique found, and what changed

Parts A to C are the design's own first pass, before the two independent critics; part D is the critique round. Part A lists what the mockup's screenshot and
collision rounds found (screenshots read at 1280, 390 and 320 px in both themes; `shoot.cjs` and its sweeps). Part B
lists what the first complete design fell short of against the bar (Stripe, Linear, Observable, NYT graphics, Bartosz
Ciechanowski) and the house prevention list, and what changed. Part C is what was considered and left out, and what is
still open for the critics.

## C. Left out, and open

- **Left out on purpose.** Range partitioning as a third placement (another picture: key order, not hash order; the
  lesson's third worked example carries it). Replica placement as marks on the ring (a third layer over the arcs and the
  keys; one sentence in the lesson). A handle to move the magnified stretch round the ring (a second focus of attention
  and a tappable mark with its hint to clear; the strip follows one fixed key that moves in four experiments). Timing a
  move (the model counts keys; the lesson gains one sentence on throttled copies).
- **Open for the critics.** (1) Even piles are short on their scale: four nodes at 25% fill a quarter of the pile area;
  the gridlines make it a chart, but a first glance may still read "small". (2) The phone stage is 1,082 px at 320 px,
  taller than Latency & Queueing's 985; the verdict's reserve (the longest story of 456) is most of the difference. (3)
  E3's act places points afresh, so its play has no travel; the band redraws and the piles re-form. (4) The stage is
  about 650 SVG elements at its heaviest (200 keys drawn twice); a play moves at most 51 pooled particles on transform
  and opacity, and the builder measures the Pixel 7 profile; if it drops frames the piles and flights go to one canvas
  from the same marks, as Load Balancing's fleet does. (5) On phones the table's percentages lose their sign to the
  row's head so 43.3 fits a 29 px column.
