# Storage Engines widget: critique items and what changed

The first round is the designer's own, made while reading the screenshots and the sweeps' reports against the
prevention list and the three house briefs. Each item is what was wrong in a draft of the mockup or the model, and what
changed. The critics' rounds go under it.

## Open for the critics

- The phone stage is 1,416 px tall (Consistency & Quorums: 1,272 to 1,331). Each engine carries its own Play, settings
  and total so a panel reads alone, but it is long.
- Two Plays, one an engine, and no single Play for both: a play of both at once would split attention on a desktop and
  run off screen on a phone.
- The challenge's goal is the LSM tree's alone, so the page size, the record size and the operation followed do not
  matter to it: one LSM configuration of 24 passes (18 settings of 432).
- Leveled against size-tiered compaction stays lesson text.
- E2's ask says a level fills after 10 merges; at the default the small top level fills after two flushes. The model
  rounds that level up, the simulation prices it, and the note says so, but the ask does not.
